Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

Lyranix Arcade is designed to collect as little information as practical.

Local storage

The arcade may use your browser's local storage to remember recently played games and interface preferences. This information stays in your browser unless you clear it.

Game content

Games run in the browser through Ruffle. Individual games may have their own technical behavior. Do not enter sensitive information into a game unless you understand why it requests that information.

Analytics

This default site contains no required analytics service. If analytics, advertising, accounts, or other third-party services are added later, this policy should be updated before deployment.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information published for Lyranix.
